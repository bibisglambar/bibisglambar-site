Meet The Artist

The Woman Behind the Glam

Bibi's love affair with beauty began in Lagos, Nigeria. Using her younger sister as her very first model, doing her hair, her makeup, and even making her clothes. What started as pure joy became a calling.

Since 2013, Bibi has been perfecting her craft. Self-taught, experience-driven, and deeply intentional in every look she creates. Formerly known as Haminahk and HMK Services LLC, her journey led her to Atlanta, Georgia, where Bibisglambar was born from one simple belief: every woman deserves to feel confident, seen, and beautiful in her own skin.

Her signature Timeless Soft Glam is rooted in a minimal or no-foundation technique, built on one priority: you should always look like yourself, only elevated. Not transformed. Not masked. Simply, undeniably you.

"I want every woman who sits in my chair to leave feeling confident, safe, and entirely satisfied. Still looking like herself, just even more beautiful than she already is."

Bibi, Founder

The Experience

Not a tutorial.
A masterclass in your own face.

This is an intimate, live demonstration — not a hands-on workshop. Bibi walks through her signature Timeless Soft Glam technique from start to finish, narrating every decision so you understand not just how, but why.

Twenty women in the room. Every seat has a clear view of the work. You'll leave knowing how to prep your skin, build a base that enhances rather than masks, and create a look that is polished, intentional, and entirely your own.

Curriculum

Three hours of technique, broken into three acts.

I

Skin, Base & Brows

Skincare prep, undertone identification, foundation alternatives, and brow technique for your face shape. The foundation of a look that photographs beautifully and lasts all day.

II

Eyes & Contour

Eyeshadow blending for your eye shape, liner technique, contour and highlight placement that reads as natural. How to make eyes look lifted, open, and intentional.

III

Lips, Lashes & Final Look

Lash application, lip liner and color for lasting wear, setting the look for longevity. A walk-through of the complete Timeless Soft Glam from beginning to finish.

Before You Book

Common questions

Who is this masterclass for?

Everyday women who want to understand their own face — beginners, women who have always struggled with makeup, and anyone who has tried to recreate a look and couldn't. No professional experience needed.

Is this hands-on or a demonstration?

This is a live demonstration masterclass. Bibi works on a model in real time while narrating every technique and decision. You observe, take notes, ask questions — and leave with a full guide to recreate the look at home.

How long does the early-bird price last?

The Atelier Seat is currently available at the Early Bird rate of $297 for a limited time. Once the offer ends, pricing returns to $347.

Where is the in-person event?

The masterclass takes place in Snellville, GA. The exact venue address will be shared with in-person ticketholders one week before the event via email.

Can I transfer my ticket?

Tickets are non-refundable. You may transfer your ticket to another person up to 14 days before November 14th. Contact bibisglambar@gmail.com to arrange a transfer.

Will the livestream be available after the event?

Signature Seat holders have access to the full recording for 30 days after the event. In-person ticket holders are not automatically granted replay access.
